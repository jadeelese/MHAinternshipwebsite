    About this Dashboard

    Created for Master of Healthcare Administration students in California throughout all phases of their academic journey.

    Inspired by FellowSource, a PharmD fellowship site created by Mark Kamel. This site serves to enhance exposure to internship and fellowship opportunities for MHA students as a resource for navigating a landscape traditionally built for MBA and MPH students.

    Dashboard Requirements

    aka eligibility rules; what counts as a listing:

    • Program, not permanent role: a structured internship or fellowship with a defined length and end date. No regular job postings.
    • Topic: any role an MHA student can realistically land, across industries. "Realistically" means a program that explicitly recruits advanced-degree holders or that CA MHA graduates have publicly entered in recent cycles — not an inferred fit.
    • Location: onsite and hybrid must be in California. Remote may be anywhere.
    • Conference travel (if required): the employer covers travel + lodging + fees.

    How to use it

    • Browse is the list. Sort is by deadline. Three quick filters at the top; City and Pay below them. Pick In-program to hide post-grad fellowships.
    • My Dashboard is your personal tracker. Check each requirement as you finish it.
    • Just added flags listings added in the last 14 days.
    • Tracking is saved in this browser only. Export a JSON to move it to another device.

    Consulting pathways

    Most consulting firms recruit summer interns through two parallel programs — undergraduate and MBA/advanced-degree candidates (MD, JD, PhD). Discuss with your campus career center which track aligns with your background and their pathways, or apply to both.

    Consulting application periods are cyclical — they occur at roughly the same time each year with similar requirements and pathways. If this is on your radar and you are enrolled in a two-year MHA program or an MHA program that begins at non-traditional times in the academic year, monitor application periods closely as consulting summer internships typically take place the summer before graduation and are applied for the year prior.

    The major consulting firms CA MHA students most often target:

    • The Big Three (MBB) — McKinsey & Company, Boston Consulting Group (BCG), and Bain & Company.
    • The Big Four — Deloitte, PwC, EY (Ernst & Young), and KPMG.

    Firms may recruit only from select partner schools. Speak with your career center to find out what consulting firms your campus has a recruitment relationship with.

    Verify before you apply

    Program deadlines move every cycle. Every listing on this site carries a last verified date; always double-check the official posting before you commit.
