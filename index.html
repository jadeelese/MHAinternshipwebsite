    About this list

    The list of fellowships and internships, by deadline. For California MHA students.

    Made for every California MHA student — new program, part-time or full-time, current or recently graduated. If it's not something a CA MHA student can realistically land, it's not on the list.

    Who this is for

    You're in an MHA program that's new enough that the internship-and-fellowship landscape is largely uncharted. You don't yet know what you don't know — and by the time you realize you should be applying, you're probably already behind on deadlines. This list exists to catch you before that happens.

    What counts as a listing

    • Program, not permanent role: a structured internship or fellowship with a defined length and end date. No regular job postings.
    • Topic: any role an MHA student can realistically land, across industries.
    • Location: onsite and hybrid must be in California. Remote may be anywhere.
    • Conference travel (when required): the employer covers travel + lodging + fees.

    How to use it

    • Browse is the list. Sort is by deadline. Three quick filters at the top; City and Pay in the drawer. Pick In-program to hide post-grad fellowships.
    • My dashboard is your personal tracker. Tick each requirement as you finish it and the little vertical stroke on the left fills.
    • Just added chips mark listings added in the last 14 days.
    • Tracking is saved in this browser only. Export a JSON to move it to another device.

    What I wish I'd known

    • Go to the in-person consulting fairs at your campus. Build relationships with recruiters and with current consultants who are alumni of your program.
    • At most consulting firms there are two parallel summer-intern tracks — one named after undergraduate roles, one for advanced-degree candidates. MHA students may fit either, depending on your path into the program.
    • Post-grad administrative fellowships at California health systems are 12–24 month paid programs that start July after graduation. Most deadlines land in fall of your final year.
    • If a program isn't posted for this cycle yet, note last year's deadline and check the official page periodically.

    Consulting pathways

    Most consulting firms recruit summer interns through two parallel programs — one for undergraduates, one for advanced-degree candidates (MBA, MHA, MD, JD, PhD). The two have different applications, different interview processes, and sometimes different URLs. Both appear on Browse. Discuss with your campus career center which one fits your profile, or apply to both.

    Verify before you apply

    Program deadlines move every cycle. Every listing on this site carries a last verified date; always double-check the official posting before you commit. If a program says their application window requires you to be at a specific school, contact your MHA program's career center to confirm.
