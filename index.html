    About this list

    The list of fellowships and internships a UCLA MHA student should actually apply to this year, tracked from deadline to offer.

    Made for the UCLA MHA cohort. Not a global job board. If it's not something a UCLA MHA student can realistically get and do — it's not on the list.

    What counts as a listing

    • Topic fit: aligned with an MHA (hospital ops, provider/payer ops, health policy, consulting, healthcare finance/strategy) — or a non-technical role in healthcare tech (EHR, EMR, CDS, HIT, healthcare AI, digital health). No SWE, ML engineering, or hard-coding roles.
    • Location fit: doable from UCLA / LA, or remote-from-LA, or UCLA-recruited.
    • Conference travel: if a program requires conference travel, the employer must cover travel + lodging + fees.

    How to use it

    • Browse is the list. Sort is by deadline. Three quick filters at the top; more in the drawer.
    • My dashboard is your personal tracker. Tick each requirement as you finish it and the little vertical stroke on the left fills. When it closes into a filled circle at the top, you've submitted.
    • Everything is saved in this browser only. Export a JSON to move it.

    Freshness

    Every listing has a last verified date. Anything older than 45 days shows a small "verify" chip that opens the source page in a new tab. Always confirm the deadline on the official source before you apply — dates shift each cycle.

    The 3% remix

    Inspired by FellowSource. Three things this list does differently: UCLA-specific curation (each listing carries a "UCLA history" chip), a requirement-first framing (your next deadline is the first thing you see), and share cards built for the cohort GroupMe.
